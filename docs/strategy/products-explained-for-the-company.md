# What we build: a plain-English guide to OGIAM's products

For everyone at the company, technical or not. After reading this, anyone here can explain what each of our products does and why a client should care, in words a non-technical person feels. If you can say it to a friend at dinner, you can say it to a client.

Each product has the same four lines: "what it is," "who it's for," "what it does for them," and "the one line to say." Skim to the product you need and use the one-liner.

---

## OGIAM in one sentence

We build software that runs the day-to-day operation of a business, keeps it safe and on-brand, and gets smarter about that business over time. Everything we ship sits on one shared, hardened foundation (more on that at the end).

---

## The products

### 1. Instinct / OGIAM: connected operations, safe automation, and trust

The platform for connecting a business's existing tools, doing the repetitive work for them, and proving every action is controlled. It does four things:

- **Connects their tools** so 6 to 10 apps that never talked now show up in one place. Ask once, get the customer, the history, and the invoice together instead of opening four screens.
- **Does the repetitive work, safely.** It follows the rulebook exactly every time (the word for that is "deterministic") and waits for a person's yes before anything important. Not a chatbot: a generic AI guesses, ours follows your rules.
- **Gets smarter every week.** It learns their language and corrections, so it gets more accurate over time and cheaper to run.
- **Controls and logs everything.** Every important action is permission-gated and recorded in a tamper-proof log, so you can always show what changed, why, and who approved it.

- **Who it's for:** med spas and aesthetics groups, auto service groups, dental (DSO) groups first; fitness and veterinary groups next.
- **The one line to say:** "We connect your systems, take the repetitive work off your team's plate safely, and get smarter about your business every week."

### 2. OGIAM Auto: a dealership's whole operation in one place

- **What it is:** one system that runs a car dealership, replacing the 8 to 12 separate tools most dealers juggle (inventory, leads, financing paperwork, service, accounting, payroll, analytics).
- **Who it's for:** independent and mid-market car dealerships and dealer groups.
- **What it does for them:** the whole store runs on one platform that learns from every interaction, with built-in checks for the compliance rules dealers must follow and its own security scanning, instead of staff copying data between a dozen disconnected apps.
- **The one line to say:** "It replaces the dozen disconnected tools a dealership runs on with one system that keeps them compliant and gets smarter over time."

### 3. Beyond: content and assets for brands, built to grow

- **What it is:** a platform for brands to produce ecommerce content and assets. Its first module, SKU Studio, creates product photography and assets with AI. It is one shared foundation that more modules plug into over time.
- **Who it's for:** brands and ecommerce teams that need a lot of high-quality product content, fast.
- **What it does for them:** turns slow, expensive asset production (like studio product photography) into something fast and repeatable, and each new module makes the others better.
- **The one line to say:** "It produces the product content brands need at scale, starting with AI product photography, on a platform built to keep adding capabilities."

### 4. Porsche Experience OS (A Weekend with Porsche): a premium experience plus a back-office console

- **What it is:** two things in one. A polished, password-gated experience a client shares with their audience, and an "Experience OS" back-office where the client's own team runs their program: their people, a program assistant that answers from their official materials, resources, and their own activity and analytics.
- **Who it's for:** premium brands and their local locations. Built first for Porsche Centers.
- **What it does for them:** a beautiful, on-brand experience for their audience and a simple console their staff run the program from, with each location's data kept private to that location.
- **The one line to say:** "A premium, on-brand experience for their audience, plus a simple console their team runs the program from."

### 5. OGIAM LMS: an AI tutor that knows when to step in

- **What it is:** a learning platform built on one idea: a tutor that steps in at the right moment is the difference between someone learning and someone giving up. It fits existing training programs (SCORM/cmi5 standards) and has deep analytics on how people actually learn.
- **Who it's for:** organizations that train people (staff, customers, partners) and want proof it is working.
- **What it does for them:** turns "watch a video and hope" into guided learning where help arrives at the right moment, and you can see exactly who is learning and where they get stuck.
- **The one line to say:** "An AI tutor that steps in at the right moment, on a platform that plugs into your training and shows you what is actually working."

### 6. AgenticQA: from "describe a feature" to "shipped," safely

- **What it is:** a platform that governs, generates, security-scans, tests, self-heals, and ships software on a controlled, repeatable cycle, with a compliance and audit layer (HIPAA, GDPR, EU AI Act) and a single simple input for non-technical users.
- **Who it's for:** teams and enterprises that want to build and ship software faster without giving up control, safety, or compliance.
- **What it does for them:** takes a feature from an idea to shipped, with security, testing, and governance built into every step, so speed does not cost safety.
- **The one line to say:** "It takes a feature from described to shipped, with security, testing, and compliance built into every step."

## The thing that ties it all together: one foundation under everything

This is worth understanding, because it is a real reason clients should trust us. We do not rebuild the plumbing for every product. We built the hard parts once, the analytics spine, the safety and content gates, the automatic testing, the safe-deploy-and-rollback machinery, and the governance, and we reuse them across every product and every client site.

Two things fall out of that, and both are client value:

- **Everything a client touches is consistent, secure, and solid from day one,** because it is all standing on the same proven foundation, not a fresh one-off.
- **Every improvement we make to the foundation improves every product at once,** so the whole portfolio gets better together.

In plain terms: this is how a focused team ships enterprise-grade software across this many products. We build it once and reuse it everywhere.

## How to talk about any of it (the habit that matters)

Lead with the outcome the client feels, not the technology:

1. **Open with the win:** what changes in their day (less busywork, one place, faster answers, the work just gets done).
2. **Then the trust:** "Every important action is controlled, logged, and explainable."
3. **Only if they raise it:** compliance and audit ("you can prove what changed, why, and who approved it").

Do not open with "governance" or "compliance." Lead with the everyday win. Trust is the closer, not the opener.

## Jargon to plain English (keep this handy)

| If someone says... | Say this instead |
|---|---|
| Deterministic automation | It follows your rules exactly, every time |
| Policy gate | A rulebook the system cannot break |
| Audit trail, tamper-evident log | A logbook no one can secretly edit |
| Closed-loop analytics | We measure what happens and feed it back to improve |
| Learning loop | It gets smarter from how your team uses it |
| Role-gated, capability-gated | Only the right people can trigger the important actions |
| Multi-tenant, data isolation | Each client's data is kept separate from every other client's |
| Shared substrate, foundation | We build the hard parts once and reuse them everywhere |

## If a client asks... (works for any of our products)

- **"Is this just ChatGPT?"** "No. ChatGPT guesses. Ours follows your rules and can safely do the work, with a person approving anything important."
- **"Will it make mistakes or go rogue?"** "It cannot act on the important things without a person's yes, and everything it does is logged."
- **"What do we get, and how fast?"** "We start with a small, paid pilot on your real workflow, and the goal is value in the first week."
- **"Is our data safe?"** "Every action is permission-controlled and recorded, and each client's data is kept separate from every other client's."
- **"Why should we trust a small team with something this important?"** "Because everything we ship stands on one hardened foundation we reuse across every product, so it is enterprise-grade from day one, not a one-off."
