# CEO brief: Vertical GTM packaging for Instinct and OGIAM

Status: internal strategy brief for CEO review. Consolidates capability scan, vertical one-pagers, positioning shift, and 90-day execution plan.

Date: 2026-07-08
Owner: product + GTM

---

## 1) Executive summary

We have stronger platform capability than most vertical AI operators. As we begin pitching these tools, we should lead with operational outcomes first, then layer in governance and compliance value.

What wins in this market:
- Lead with operational outcomes the buyer feels this week
- Prove value with a short fixed pilot
- Keep governance as the trust and expansion moat

Recommended shift:
- First pitch: connected operations and deterministic automation outcomes
- Second pitch: governance, auditability, and compliance proof

This preserves our moat and improves conversion with non-technical buyers.

---

## 2) Market signal from successful vertical operators

Successful vertical operators tend to win by doing three things well:
1. They sell a daily operator outcome in plain language
2. They deploy in a narrow, repeatable environment first
3. They hide infrastructure complexity behind a simple operational story

Implication for us:
- We should not lead with policy engine details in SMB or mid-market outbound
- We should lead with the measurable workflow wins and show governance as built in

---

## 3) Our actual capability strength (what we can credibly claim)

### Connected systems and cross-tool intelligence
- Unified assistant layer across Microsoft 365, CRM, GitHub, finance, and internal docs
- Cross-tool insight tooling and pending-action workflows
- Search and action paths that work across systems, not in silos

### Deterministic automation
- Tool-driven deterministic handlers for critical actions
- Confirmation and pending-action lifecycle for safe execution
- Capability and role-gated actions

### Compounding intelligence and cost efficiency
- Workspace learning loop from user feedback and corrections
- Retrieval-first architecture with caching
- Cost and latency improve over time as usage history grows

### Governance and trust moat
- Deterministic policy gate
- Tamper-evident audit and decision trail
- Evidence-ready posture for regulated and enterprise expansion

Bottom line: we can credibly claim that we improve operations now and compound value over time.

## 5) Commercial packaging recommendation

### Offer structure
- 14-day fixed-scope pilot
- One to two deterministic automations
- Connected data layer for selected workflows
- Baseline insight report with ROI and rollout plan

### Pricing posture
- Keep fixed pilot pricing by vertical range
- Use monthly production rollout after pilot
- Do not custom-build pre-sale

### Delivery rule
- Demo with reusable vertical shell and sample data
- Build client-specific connectors and workflow depth only after paid pilot

---

## 6) Messaging hierarchy for CEO approval

### Primary message (opening)
"We connect your existing systems, automate high-friction workflows deterministically, and improve your operation every week as the system learns your business."

### Secondary message (trust and moat)
"Every critical action is controlled, logged, and explainable."

### Tertiary message (enterprise and compliance expansion)
"You can prove what changed, why it changed, and who approved it."

Do not open with compliance in SMB motion unless the buyer explicitly raises it first.

- Documented operational ROI metric per pilot: required

## 10) Key risks and mitigations

### Risk: messaging still too technical
Mitigation: enforce operations-first copy in every first-touch asset

### Risk: custom pre-sale build creep
Mitigation: no custom code before signed pilot

### Risk: weak proof of value
Mitigation: pilot must include baseline and endline metrics

### Risk: pricing confusion
Mitigation: fixed pilot, simple monthly rollout tiers
