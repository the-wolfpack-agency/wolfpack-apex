# SOW addendum: Wolfpack Instinct setup + 90-day evaluation

> Drop-in attachment for any Wolfpack Agency SOW of $50,000 or higher. Bundles Instinct setup as a value-add deliverable and bakes the post-engagement subscription expectation into the contract from day one. Updated 2026-05-16.

## Usage notes for the Wolfpack team (DELETE BEFORE SENDING)

This addendum exists so the client knows two things up front:
1. They are receiving Instinct workspace setup as part of the engagement at no separate charge.
2. After the 90-day evaluation period, continued use is paid via subscription. Opt-out is one email, no penalty.

Without this clause, the default client assumption is "the tool came with the project, so it's mine forever." That assumption is what kills the SaaS conversion. The addendum reframes Instinct as a trial seeded by the engagement.

**Pre-built pairings.** Each engagement type maps to a specific Instinct surface so setup is repeatable, not custom every time. Pick the row that matches the engagement and copy the matching scope language into Section 3 below.

| Engagement type | Instinct surface bundled | Pre-built setup time |
|---|---|---|
| Brand identity build | Brand-book knowledge base + ask-the-brand-book chat | 4 hours |
| Marketing campaign | Campaign analytics + CRM connector + weekly insight digest | 6 hours |
| Website / app build | GitHub connector + CMS ingest + non-engineer change digest | 4 hours |
| Dealer audit | Audit-findings KB + dealer CRM connector + gap-monitoring report | 6 hours |
| Sales enablement | CRM connector + battle-card KB + sales-rep chat | 4 hours |

If the engagement does not match a row above, do not bundle Instinct. Either scope it separately or sell it after the engagement closes.

### Addendum A: Wolfpack Instinct workspace, setup and evaluation period

**Client:** [CLIENT LEGAL NAME]
**Effective:** [SOW START DATE]
**Parent SOW:** [SOW REFERENCE NUMBER]

#### A.1 What is included

As part of the engagement described in the parent SOW, Wolfpack Agency will provide Client with:

1. A dedicated Wolfpack Instinct workspace, provisioned and configured for Client's organization.
2. Integration setup for the following systems (the "Bundled Integration"):
   [PICK ONE FROM THE PAIRINGS TABLE: Brand-book KB / Campaign analytics + CRM / GitHub + CMS / Dealer audit + CRM / CRM + battle-cards]
3. Up to [N] named user seats during the Evaluation Period.
4. Training session for Client's designated administrator. One hour, remote.
5. Access to written documentation and email-based support during the Evaluation Period.

#### A.2 Evaluation Period

The "Evaluation Period" is the ninety (90) calendar days starting on the Effective Date above. During the Evaluation Period:
- Use of Instinct is included in the engagement fee. No additional charge.
- Wolfpack will respond to support requests within one business day.
- Client retains full ownership of any data, documents, or content Client uploads to or generates within Instinct. Wolfpack uses Client data only to operate Instinct on Client's behalf.

#### A.3 Conversion to subscription

At least fifteen (15) days before the Evaluation Period ends, Wolfpack will send Client written notice (email is sufficient) summarizing:
- Recommended subscription tier (Starter, Pro, or Managed)
- Per-seat or flat monthly fee
- Proposed start date for paid use

Client has three options at conversion:

1. **Continue:** Sign the attached subscription order form. Paid use begins the day after the Evaluation Period ends.
2. **Pause:** Request workspace suspension. Client retains data; access can be restored on payment of any standard subscription fee.
3. **Off-board:** Email Wolfpack to terminate. Within fifteen (15) days of termination, Wolfpack will export Client data in the formats listed in Section A.5 and delete Client's workspace.

Failure to respond before the end of the Evaluation Period defaults to Option 2 (Pause). Wolfpack will not auto-bill or extract a subscription fee without an explicit signed order form. This is intentional. We never want a client surprised by a charge.

#### A.4 Subscription pricing

Subscription pricing in effect on the Effective Date above:

| Tier | What it includes | Price |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | Microsoft 365 integration only. No CRM. Up to [N] seats. | $12 / seat / month |
| Pro | Everything in Starter, plus CRM connector, structured action forms, conversation history, learning loop, priority support. | $35 / seat / month |
| Managed | Everything in Pro, plus dedicated success engineer, custom connector builds, brand-customized chat surface, compliance support. | Custom, starts at $12,500 / month |

Pricing is locked for twelve (12) months from the date of conversion. Wolfpack will give Client sixty (60) days written notice before any price change after that window.

#### A.5 Data ownership and off-boarding

1. All data Client uploads or generates in Instinct is and remains Client's property.
2. On termination or off-boarding, Wolfpack will provide Client with:
   - Conversation history export (JSON and PDF)
   - Knowledge base file export (original file formats, where preserved)
   - Audit log export (CSV)
3. Wolfpack will delete all Client data from production systems within thirty (30) days of off-boarding. Backups are purged on standard 90-day rotation.

#### A.6 Confidentiality and security

Existing confidentiality, security, and data-processing terms in the parent SOW apply to Instinct workspace data. The Bundled Integration does not transfer Client data to any third party other than the platform vendor required for the integration to function (e.g., Salesforce for a Salesforce connector). Vendor list available on request.

#### A.7 Limitation of liability

Wolfpack's liability arising from Instinct use is capped at the lesser of (a) total subscription fees paid by Client in the twelve months preceding the claim, or (b) the cap stated in the parent SOW. For the avoidance of doubt, during the Evaluation Period, Wolfpack's liability is capped at $1,000.

#### A.8 No obligation to continue

For clarity: Client is under no obligation to convert to a paid subscription. Off-boarding at the end of the Evaluation Period does not affect the parent SOW deliverables, fees, or any ongoing engagement scope. Instinct is an addition to the engagement, not a condition of it.
