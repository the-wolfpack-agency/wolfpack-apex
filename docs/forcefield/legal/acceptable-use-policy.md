This AUP is incorporated into the Terms of Service. Breach may result in
suspension or termination.

## 1. You may only protect sites you own or control
Forcefield may be pointed only at sites, domains, and endpoints the customer owns
or is authorized to operate. Registering or protecting a site you do not control
is prohibited. (This mirrors the target-ownership posture used elsewhere in the
platform.)

## 2. No using the service to attack or disrupt others
Forcefield is a defense product. It may not be used to launch, facilitate, or
disguise attacks, scraping, credential stuffing, denial of service, or
unauthorized testing against any third party.

## 3. No circumventing fair use or isolation
- Do not share, resell, or reuse an ingest token across sites outside your plan,
  or to exceed the fair-use allowance by splitting traffic across tokens.
- Do not attempt to access another tenant's data, probe the isolation boundary,
  or reverse engineer the engine or rulesets.

## 4. No unlawful or harmful content/use
Do not use the service in violation of law, to process data you are not permitted
to process, or to protect a property whose own purpose is unlawful.

## 5. Respect the honeypot and detection mechanics
Do not expose, republish, or help others evade Forcefield trap paths, decoys, or
detection signatures. Doing so degrades protection for every customer on the
shared ruleset.
